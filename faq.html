Pricing

Why isn't there one fixed price per service?
Jobs of the same service differ in size. Instead of one price for all, we publish tiers with written criteria. Your answers decide your tier, the same way for everyone.
Can I get a discount?
We don't give private discounts. Any promotion is public, open to everyone and has a start and end date.
How long is a quote valid?
14 days from the date it's issued. [Owner to confirm]
What if your prices change after I've accepted a quote?
You pay the price on your accepted quote. Later price changes don't affect it.
Are government fees included?
No. Government fees, taxes, stamp duty and challans are shown on separate lines at actual cost and paid by you directly.

Orders and payment

How do I pay?
Bank transfer (with proof upload), debit or credit card, JazzCash or Easypaisa, and Raast where available. All amounts are in PKR.
Can I save my order and finish later?
Yes, orders can be saved as drafts.
What file types can I upload?
PDF, JPG and PNG. A clear phone photo is fine.
What is your refund policy?
See our Refund Policy.

Notices

I've received a notice. What should I do first?
Find the response deadline on the notice and tell us. Matters due within 7 days are flagged to the owner immediately.
How are appeals priced?
By stage. Each stage starts only after you approve and pay for it, so you're never committed to more than the next step.
