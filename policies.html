Fee Transparency Policy

  1. Published formula. Every service shows its tiers, units, stages and add-ons, with the criteria for each.
  2. No private discounts. No client gets a different price for the same inputs. Promotions are public, open to everyone and dated.
  3. Quote before payment. You see a line-by-line breakdown and the reason for your tier before paying. Quotes are numbered and valid for a set period.
  4. Price lock. The price on your accepted quote is the price you pay.
  5. Re-tiering with consent. If your documents show different facts, we pause and send a revised quote. Work resumes only after you accept it in writing.
  6. Government fees separate. Government fees, stamp duty, challans and filing charges are shown separately at actual cost and are never part of our fee.
  7. Stage approval. For staged work, the next stage starts only after you approve and pay for it.
  8. Custom scope. Work that fits no tier is priced at our public rate card and approved by you before it starts.
  9. Invoice equals quote. Your invoice matches your accepted quote exactly.
  10. Regular review. We review prices every quarter and show the last review date on the price list. Every change is logged.
