Pricing models

Five ways a price is worked out

Each service uses one of these. The model is shown on every service page.

T

Tiered price

Registrations and annual returns. Your answers place the job in a published tier such as Basic, Standard or Complex.

U

Per-unit price

Stamp duty challans and notice replies. A price per challan, instrument or notice type.

M

Monthly plan

Monthly sales tax and SRB/PRA returns. Priced by invoices per month and number of authorities.

S

Stage-based price

Audits, appeals and petitions. Each stage has its own price and starts only after you approve and pay for it.

+

Add-ons

Urgent handling, extra revisions, historic years and extra consultation, each with a published price.

C

Custom scope

If a job fits no tier, we write a scope priced at our public rate card. You approve it before work starts.

When facts change

What happens if your documents show something different

Say you told us 60 transactions a month and your documents show 300. We don't quietly charge more.

  1. We pause workNothing more is done on your order.
  2. We send a revised quoteIn the correct published tier, with the reason written out.
  3. You decideAccept the revised quote, or ask about cancelling under our refund policy.
  4. Everything is recordedYour original quote, the reason and your acceptance are all kept.
