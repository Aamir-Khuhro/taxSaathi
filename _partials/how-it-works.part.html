Pricing models

Six ways a price is worked out

Some work costs the same for everyone. Other work depends on the size and complexity of your case. Each service page tells you which, and what it depends on.

F

Fixed price

Nil returns (Individual, AOP, Company), consultations and stamp duty challans. One published price, whatever your business.

T

Tiered price

Registrations (FBR, SECP, SRB, PRA, EOBI) and annual returns for individuals, AOPs, single member, private, public and listed companies. Turnover, transactions, members or assets place the job in Basic, Standard or Complex.

N

Priced by notice type

Notices from FBR, SRB, PRA, SECP, EOBI and the Board of Revenue. An information request costs less than a show-cause or audit notice.

M

Monthly plan

Monthly sales tax returns with FBR, SRB or PRA. Priced by invoices per month and number of authorities.

S

Stage-based price

Audits, stay applications and appeals. Each stage has its own price and starts only after you approve and pay for it.

+

Add-ons and custom scope

Urgent handling, revisions, earlier years and extra consultation each have a published price. A job that fits no tier is priced at our public rate card, and you approve it first.

When facts change

  4. Everything is recordedYour original quote, the reason and your acceptance are all kept.
