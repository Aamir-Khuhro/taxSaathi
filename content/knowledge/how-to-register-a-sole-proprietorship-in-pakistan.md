## Who this is for

You run, or are about to start, a business that you own on your own: a shop, a trading business, a workshop, an online store or a professional practice. You have no partners and you have not formed a company.

## The short answer

A sole proprietorship is not a separate legal person. It is you, doing business. So it is registered with FBR under **your own NTN**: your personal tax record on IRIS is updated with your business name, the nature of the business, its address and its start date. If you do not have an NTN yet, the registration and the business details are done together.

## Steps

1. **Check your mobile number and email.** FBR sends codes to the mobile number and email on the application. The mobile number should be registered in your own name, on your own CNIC.
2. **Decide the business details.** The business name, what the business does (its main activity), the address of the premises and the date the business started.
3. **Collect the premises documents.** A recent utility bill for the premises, and either a rent agreement or proof that you own it.
4. **Register or update your NTN record on IRIS.** The application adds the business to your registration. You confirm the codes sent to your phone and email yourself; never share them with anyone by message.
5. **Keep the registration record.** Banks usually ask for it when you open a business bank account.

## Documents needed

- CNIC
- Business name, nature of business and start date
- Business address
- Utility bill for the premises
- Rent agreement or proof of ownership
- Bank account details
- Mobile number and email

## Common mistakes

- **A mobile number in someone else's name.** It can hold up the registration. Use a number registered on your own CNIC.
- **An address that does not match the utility bill.** Use the premises address exactly as it appears on the bill or the rent agreement.
- **Choosing the wrong main activity.** The activity you declare should describe what the business actually does. It matters later for your return and for any sales tax registration.
- **Thinking registration makes you a filer.** You appear on the Active Taxpayers List only after you file your income tax return.
- **Forgetting other registrations.** Selling taxable goods can mean federal sales tax registration with FBR. Providing taxable services can mean registration with SRB in Sindh or PRA in Punjab. These are separate from the FBR business registration.

## Deadlines

There is no single deadline for registering, but register before you start trading in earnest, open a business bank account or issue invoices. Once registered, your business income goes in your annual income tax return. For individuals the usual due date is 30 September for the tax year ending on 30 June, unless FBR extends it. ## AOP or sole proprietorship?

If two or more people will own the business together, a sole proprietorship is not the right form. A partnership is registered as an Association of Persons (AOP), which has its own NTN. See [AOP registration](/services/aop-registration/).
