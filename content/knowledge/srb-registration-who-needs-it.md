## Who this is for

You provide services, not just goods, and you do business in Sindh. For example: consultancy, IT and software services, advertising, contractors, event management, security, courier, or similar work.

## The short answer

Sales tax on **services** in Sindh is collected by the Sindh Revenue Board (SRB), not by FBR. If the services you provide are taxable under Sindh sales tax law and you provide them in or from Sindh, you are generally required to register with SRB and file monthly SRB returns. FBR registration for sales tax on goods is a separate thing. Some businesses need both.

## How to tell if you need it

- **Look at what you actually provide.** The law lists the services that are taxable. Your service needs to fall under one of those descriptions.
- **Look at where you provide it.** Services provided from an office or business in Sindh generally fall under SRB. Services provided in Punjab fall under the Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA) instead.
- **Look at your clients' requests.** Many companies ask their suppliers for an SRB registration number before they pay invoices. That is often the first sign you need it.

If you are not sure how your service is classified, that is the first thing to settle, because it decides the rate and the return you file.

## Steps

1. **Make sure you have an NTN.** SRB registration builds on your FBR registration.
2. **List the services you provide.** Describe each one plainly; the classification is checked against it.
3. **Collect the documents** listed below.
4. **Apply on the SRB e-services system.** The application includes your business details, premises and services.
5. **Receive your registration** and note your monthly filing dates.

## Documents needed

- NTN
- CNIC of owner, partners or directors
- Business address with utility bill
- Rent agreement or ownership proof
- List of taxable services you provide
- Bank account details
- Partnership deed or incorporation certificate where applicable

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming FBR registration covers services.** It does not cover sales tax on services in Sindh.
- **Describing services vaguely.** A vague description can lead to the wrong classification and the wrong rate.
- **Registering and then not filing.** Once registered, a monthly return is due even in a month with no sales.
- **Ignoring work in another province.** Services provided in Punjab may need PRA registration as well.
